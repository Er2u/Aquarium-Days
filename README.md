## English

### About the game

Aquarium Days is a modern, cozy take on classic aquarium-management games. In Chapter 1, you begin with a modest home aquarium: feed your fish, collect coin bubbles, expand the tank, and respond as oxygen and water quality come under pressure. Plants, cleaner fish, and equipment gradually help the aquarium become stable enough to run with less intervention.

The focus is not combat. It is the rhythm of caring for living creatures, watching a tiny ecosystem develop, and finding satisfying ways to optimize it.

### Demo highlights

- Seven fish species with individual growth, experience, rarity, value, breeding, predation, locking, and lifecycle systems.
- Coin-bubble income with manual collection and an unlockable auto collector.
- Oxygen and water-quality management, plus plants, food, filters, and oxygen pumps.
- Shop, inventory, six-slot quick bar, Fish Catalog, in-game Wiki, and aquarium editing.
- Three save slots with backup recovery and display-setting persistence.
- A compact Chapter 1 progression designed to move from hands-on care toward a stable, semi-automated aquarium.

### Download and play

1. Download [`Aquarium-Days-Chapter-1-MVP-Windows-x64.zip`](https://github.com/Er2u/Aquarium-Days/releases/latest/download/Aquarium-Days-Chapter-1-MVP-Windows-x64.zip).
2. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder. Do not run the game from inside the ZIP.
3. Launch `Aquarium Days.exe`. Keep the executable, `Aquarium Days_Data`, and the other extracted files together.

No installation is required.

### Controls

- **Left mouse button:** interact, collect coin bubbles, select, place, and confirm.
- **1–6 / Numpad 1–6:** select a quick-bar slot.
- **Right mouse button or Esc:** cancel feeding, placement, editing, or quick-bar assignment.
- The main on-screen buttons open the shop, Fish Catalog, inventory, aquarium editor, Wiki, pause menu, and settings.

### Demo status

This is an early MVP build, not a finished release. Features, balance, UI, content, and save compatibility may change. The current in-game interface is Simplified Chinese only.

## 中文

### 游戏介绍

《Aquarium Days》是一款现代、治愈的水族箱经营游戏。Chapter 1 从一个普通家庭鱼缸开始：给鱼喂食、点击收集金币泡泡、购买更多鱼和物品，并在氧气与水质压力出现后逐步调整生态。植物、清洁型鱼类和设备会帮助鱼缸稳定下来，让玩家从亲手照料慢慢过渡到半自动运转。

游戏的核心不是战斗，而是照顾生命、观察小型生态逐渐成形，以及用轻松但有深度的方式持续优化鱼缸。

### 试玩内容

- 7 种 Chapter 1 鱼类，包含个体成长、经验、稀有度、价值、生育、捕食、锁定和生命周期。
- 鱼类产出金币泡泡，支持手动点击收集和可解锁的自动收集器。
- 氧气与水质管理，以及植物、鱼食、过滤器和小型氧气泵。
- 商店、库存、6 格快捷栏、鱼目录、游戏内 Wiki 和鱼缸编辑模式。
- 3 个存档槽，包含备份恢复与显示设置保存。
- 从手动照顾逐步过渡到稳定、半自动鱼缸的 Chapter 1 核心流程。

### 下载与运行

1. 下载 [`Aquarium-Days-Chapter-1-MVP-Windows-x64.zip`](https://github.com/Er2u/Aquarium-Days/releases/latest/download/Aquarium-Days-Chapter-1-MVP-Windows-x64.zip)。
2. 将 ZIP 完整解压到一个文件夹中，请勿直接在压缩包内运行。
3. 启动 `Aquarium Days.exe`。请保持可执行文件、`Aquarium Days_Data` 和其他解压文件位于同一目录。

无需安装。

### 操作方式

- **鼠标左键：** 交互、收集金币泡泡、选择、放置和确认。
- **数字键 1–6 / 小键盘 1–6：** 选择快捷栏槽位。
- **鼠标右键或 Esc：** 取消投喂、放置、编辑或快捷栏设置。
- 通过画面中的主按钮打开商店、鱼目录、库存、编辑鱼缸、Wiki、暂停菜单和设置。

### 试玩版说明

这是早期 MVP 试玩构建，并非正式完成版。功能、数值、UI、内容和存档兼容性都可能继续调整。当前游戏内界面仅提供简体中文。
